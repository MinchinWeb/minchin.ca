[MyrAir](https://sites.google.com/view/myrair) offers ~$500 million in
financing, at 0%. I've wondered if the real money to be made is people that get
excited, lease a bigger plane that they should, and then disappear in 6 months
and just let the "bank foreclose" on the loan. If you run the loan side, all
that money that they've put towards the plane is now yours. MyrAir asks for a
20% down payment.
