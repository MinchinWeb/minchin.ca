Does it make sense to buy a plane? You can generally rent one, and rent is
usually about 1/1,000th of the purchase price. But if you own a plane, you can
control where it is. That said, you are now tied to the plane's location for
your next job (unless you want to go rent something else).

**XX** Can you make money renting a plane?

If you own a plane, you are responsible for 100 hour checks, and random
maintenance.
