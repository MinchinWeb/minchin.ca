Located east of Airdrie, Alberta, Canada, just north of Calgary International
(CYYC).

IRL (and in MSFS 2024), it was converted to a single runway, but it exists in
FSE under the "triangle" layout from when it was built during World War II. The
bonus is it has a far larger size in FSE than the real life counterpart would
typically suggest.

I bought it as a base to build an Alberta network. It's about the right
distance to [Banff (CYBA)]({filename}cyba.md) (~60 nm), but a little further
than I'd like to [Josephburg (CFB6)]({filename}cfb6.md) (~150 nm) for light
planes.

Paid $150k for it in September 2025.
