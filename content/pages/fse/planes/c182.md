## In FSEconomy

In FSE, it carries (up to) 3 passengers, and can be had from ~v$150,000. The
monthly cost for an upgraded model is ~v$800. Compare with the [Cessna 172]({filename}pages/fse/planes/c172.md).

## In Microsoft Flight Simulator

The plane is not included in the MSFS 2024 (payware versions are available), however, included in my [Cessna 185 FSE Tanks]( ihttps://github.com/minchinweb/msfs-airports/releases/tag/c185-v0.1.7)
is a variant that matches the FSEconomy 182. So this will allow you to fly thr 185 in sim, but havr FSEconomy count it as a 182 flight.
Read the page on the [Cessna 185]({filename}pages/fse/planes/c185.md)
for details on tips on flying it.
