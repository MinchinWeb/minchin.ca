One of the most popular planes in FSE, probably partly because it is one of the most used General Aviation and trainer planes in the real world, and it included in most simulators for this reason.

In FSE, the plane seats 3, and so works well for servicing 1-lot airfields.

Saw listed for v$100k, and they were snapped up within hours. More tupical prices start at v$150k.

- [Titan](https://fseconomy.net/forum/mp-aircraft/147800-titan-air-leasing-super-competitive-prices) offers one for lease at v$2,000/month
