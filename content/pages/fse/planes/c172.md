## In FSEconomy

In FSE, the plane seats 3, and so works well for servicing 1-lot airfields
(which generate up to 3 passengers).

I have seen them listed for v$100k and they were snapped up within hours. More
typical prices start at v$150k.

One of the most popular planes in FSE, probably partly because it is one of the
most used General Aviation and trainer planes in the real world, and it
included in most simulators for this reason.

If you're looking for a something 1/3 of the price but only carries 2
passengers, consider the [Cessna 152]({filename}c152.md).
The [Cessna 182]({filename}c182.md) is very similiar, but less popular (and so probably cheaper).
If you're looking for
something little bigger, consider the [Cessna 185]({filename}c185.md),
which seats 5 passenger at half again the purchase price.

## In Microsoft Flight Simulator

Fixed landing gear.

In MSFS, the base game includes this with a glass cockpit (the G1000). It thus
includes a very capable GPS navigation system. The further bonus is that is you
learn this setup, the same G1000 is used in the [Cessna
208]({filename}c208.md). In general, it flight very much like the [Cessna
152]({filename}c152.md): slow landing speeds, low stall speeds, and generally
very forgiving. Respectable cruise speed of ~100 kts (or a hair higher). It's
fuel tank is twice that of the 152, and so it gets twice the range (in FSE, or
770 nm)!
