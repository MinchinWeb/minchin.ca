## In FSEconomy

In FSEconomy, the plane seats 14 or can carry 2,604 kg of cargo. It has a
(nominal) cruise speed of 258 knt.

FSEconomy pricing seems to start (as of September 2025) at ~v$730,000.

## In Microsoft Flight Simulator

Shows up in MSFS 2024 as the "Textron Aviation Beechcraft King Air 350i". Fuel
tank match to FSEconomy is almost perfect, so don't fill past ~99%.

As a twin prop, it easily cruises at 180 knt (not quite double the [Cessna
208]({filename}c208.md)).

It does have (deployable) landing gear, so you need to remember to pull it up
once off the ground, and drop it before landing.

In MSFS 2024, I have yet to figure out how to work the flight computer. It is
similar to the G1000 (in the [Cessna 172]({filename}c172.md) and
[208]({filename}c208.md)), but the controls are between the two seats on a
lower console. In a real plane, that probably puts them very handy, but in the
simulator, I haven't figured out how to have the display and the controls
visible at the same time. I'm also not sure how thoroughly simulated the flight
computer is, as it didn't seem to pick up the flight plans from my (in-game)
tablet, which makes the AutoPilot of somewhat limited use (as it can't just
follow the pre-programmed route).

As well, the plane has a fairly tall "dash", so I need to find the slew
controls for MSFS so I can "look over the dash".

Similar planes include the [Cessna CJ4]({filename}c25c.md).
