The DC-3 is a workhorse or another era. In FSE, it catries 19 passengers, which can make it challenging to fill without some planning. That said, it seems to be cheap enough to operate that it turns decent profit for me even half full. As a prop plane, it's not particularly fast, but its not slow for what it is. Once off the ground, it climbs well.

Has been included in MSFS 2024 as part of thr base game; fuel tanks match the FSE layout.

Can be had starting at v$850,000, although all the currently (as of March 11, 2025) for sale models have hundreds of hours on them.
