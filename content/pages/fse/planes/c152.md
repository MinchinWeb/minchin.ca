Fixed landing gear.

In FSE, it carries (up to) 2 passengers, and can be had from ~$50,000[^1]. The
monthly cost for an upgraded model is ~$400, which makes it a very light carry.

In MSFS, flying with the JP Logistics model adds a basic autopilot, which makes
flying over any distance much more enjoyable. The plane is typically presented
with "steam" instruments (rather than a "glass cockpit"), which I like the
charm of. The plane is light, and so is sensitive to being tossed around in the
wind. There is also a "base model" included with the game. Respectable cruise
speed of ~100 kts. In general, very forgiving to fly.

Regarding the third seat (that doesn't seem to exist in "real life" models):
