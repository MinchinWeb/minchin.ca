This is probably the workhorse of FSE. It carries 13 passengers, which sits in
a sweet spot of not to big to actually fill, but large enough to bring in
considerable amounts of money. However, it's size doesn't particularly lend
itself to servicing 1-lot FBOs without planning. I have read that 2x 2-lot FBOs
pointed at each other with a Cessna 208 to fly the jobs between them is a great
money making option.

Included in the base game of MSFS. It include a G1000 "glass cockpit". In
general, it's rather forgiving to fly with low take off and stall speeds. Fixed
landing gear. Not hugely fast, with a cruise speed of ~140 kts.

Can be had starting at ~$550,000 or down to $500,000 if you're lucky. Leases are
typically $20-25,000/month, but I have seen as low as $10,000/month. Monthly cost
is ~$4,500/month.
